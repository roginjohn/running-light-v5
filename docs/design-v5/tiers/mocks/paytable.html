Double Run

About 1 in 25 rounds, a second light runs and stops on a different square. Each light pays on its own, so both lights on your tier pay twice.

Limits

  • Up to 1,000 on each tier spot and 1,000 per round
  • Chips 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 500
  • Start with 10,000 play credits; free refill to 10,000
  • Gamble is off above 300,000
  • Simulated credits only, no real money
